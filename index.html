No drones. No experience. Not even a team.

Someone looked around my coworking space and asked four people if they were up for something none of them knew anything about.

Everyone said yes.

Why not.

Five software engineers. Five completely different backgrounds. Zero aerospace. No deck, no masterplan — just one question that wouldn’t let us go: how far can you actually get if you take AI seriously?

The arena: the MBDA & brigkAIR Swarm Drone Challenge 2026. Not a university project. Not a hackathon. Teams from around the world, with real experience in autonomous systems.

We signed up eight weeks before the qualification deadline.

The qualification: one drone, one target, autonomous. A lot of people didn’t think we’d make it through.

We made it through.

But that was just the beginning.

Because in the final, you don’t just fly. You play.

Swarm versus swarm. Capture the Flag. Fully autonomous. Once the mission starts, no human touches the controls.

The AI decides the strategy. Live. On the field. It watches the opposing swarm, reads the situation, picks its tactics — and executes.

Side note: two of our developers had family vacations booked over Whitsun. The final was three days after they got back.

Family comes first. That was clear to all of us.

So we set up a remote PC. Develop on the beach in the evenings. The rest of the team tests live in the hall at home.

Top 6. International. ILA Berlin. Defence Track.
